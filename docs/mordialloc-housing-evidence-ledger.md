## Research rules

- Never infer a candidate's position solely from party membership.
- Clearly label party-level evidence.
- Prefer official and primary sources.
- Record the actual source URL, not a search-results URL.
- Preserve relevant excerpts accurately.
- Do not count duplicated or syndicated material as independent evidence.
- Explicitly record when searches produce no usable evidence.
- No score is assigned until the evidence is evaluated.
- No assessment is published without review.
