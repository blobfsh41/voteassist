# Mordialloc Policy Assessment Pilot

## Objective

Test the complete VoteAssist candidate-policy assessment process using the Mordialloc electorate before scaling to additional electorates.

## Scope

- Electorate: Mordialloc
- Election: 2026 Victorian State Election
- Candidates: All 8 nominated candidates
- Initial policy: Housing supply
- Initial target: 8 assessments

## Candidates

1. Paul O'Halloran — Liberal
2. Mahee Hossain — Victorian Socialists
3. Lily Potucek — Family First
4. Rachel Versteegen — Libertarian (formerly Liberal Democrats)
5. Nadia Ticehurst — One Nation Victoria
6. Alex Breskin — The Greens
7. Tim Richardson — Australian Labor Party
8. Renee Thompson — Legalise Cannabis Victoria

## Policy proposition

Planning rules should be changed to allow more housing to be built in areas with high housing demand.

## Assessment rules

- Assess each candidate independently.
- Do not assign positions based solely on party affiliation.
- Party policy may be used as evidence but must be identified as party-level evidence.
- A candidate-specific position requires candidate-specific evidence.
- If evidence is insufficient, assign null.
- Never assign 0 merely because a candidate has no known position.
- Record sources, publication dates, excerpts, rationale and confidence.
- Do not publish assessments until they have passed validation and independent review.

## Pilot completion criteria

- [ ] All 8 candidates accounted for.
- [ ] Evidence search completed and documented for each candidate.
- [ ] Assessments supported by traceable evidence.
- [ ] Insufficient evidence explicitly recorded where applicable.
- [ ] Assessments validated.
- [ ] Review process completed.
- [ ] No unreviewed assessments published.

## Expansion criteria

Do not expand to additional policies until the pilot demonstrates that the assessment process is reliable, repeatable, transparent and practical.
